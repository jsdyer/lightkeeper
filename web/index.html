Sources & coverage

Lightkeeper indexes positioned, lit entries from the Pacific, Atlantic and Newfoundland and Labrador volumes of the Canadian Coast Guard’s List of Lights, Buoys and Fog Signals. Buoys, towers, inland waterways and sector lights can all appear. Each record links to its section and gives the section correction date. Dates differ between sections.

Educational exploration only. For navigation, consult official current charts, publications and notices. Observing a light in the field also depends on the viewing direction, visibility and operational status.

Contains information published by the Canadian Coast Guard and Department of Fisheries and Oceans licensed under the Canadian Coast Guard Open Information Licence. This Information is available in its original format for free download on the Canadian Coast Guard website and the Monthly Edition of the Notices to Mariners (NOTMAR) website.
